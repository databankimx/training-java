## Homework

These exercises practise writing recursive methods with a base case and a recursive case: summing numbers in a nested list, and raising a number to a power. Each one is a step in this folder.

The steps are:

* [Sum nested numbers](#goto/01-sum-nested-numbers): adding every number in a list that may contain lists.
* [Recursive power](#goto/02-recursive-power): raising a number to a power by multiplying with the result for one less.

Write your own program in your project first. If you get stuck, reveal the example. The source shows the finished program, and the section below says which lines to change for your own version.
