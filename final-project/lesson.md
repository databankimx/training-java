## Final project

The final project brings the course together in one program: a calculator that reads expressions such as `2 + 3 * (4 - 1)` and prints the result. It uses classes, enums, records, methods, loops, switch expressions, exceptions with clear messages, recursion, and self-tests.

The project is in the homework folder, in two steps. The core is the calculator itself. The bonus adds exponents, square roots, and more kinds of brackets.

---

*The project is not from the book. It uses ideas from Chapters 1 to 34.*
