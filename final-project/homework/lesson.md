## Homework

The final project is a calculator, and it's built in two steps. The core is first, and the bonus items come in the second, since each bonus item builds on the core.

The steps are:

* [The calculator core](#goto/01-calculator): the tokenizer, the parser, the REPL, and the demo and self-test modes.
* [The calculator bonus](#goto/02-calculator-bonus): exponents, square roots, and matching square and curly brackets.

Write your own program in your project first. The reference program in each step stays hidden until you reveal it, and the walkthrough follows it.
