## Homework

These exercises practise counting operations and comparing the cost of two ways of doing the same job. Each one is a step in this folder.

The steps are:

* [Count pairs](#goto/03-count-pairs): nested loops that count pairs, checked against a formula.
* [Stairs](#goto/04-stairs): counting the calls of a recursive definition, and comparing it with a memoized version.

Write your own program in your project first. If you get stuck, reveal the example. The source shows the finished program, and the section below says which lines to change for your own version.
