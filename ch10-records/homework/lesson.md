## Homework

These exercises use records for values that belong together: a rectangle with a checked size, and a comparison of records by their values. Each one is a step in this folder.

The steps are:

* [Rectangle](#goto/01-rectangle): a record with a compact constructor that rejects a zero or negative size, and an `area` method.
* [Same place](#goto/02-same-place): records compared with `equals`, and a record that is used as a key for a comparison.

Write your own program in your project first. If you get stuck, reveal the example. The source shows the finished program, and the section below says which lines to change for your own version.
