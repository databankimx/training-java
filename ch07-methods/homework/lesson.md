## Homework

These exercises practise methods: calculating areas, and computing factorials two ways. Each one is a step in this folder.

The steps are:

* [Area functions](#goto/03-area-functions): two methods that calculate areas, used from `main`.
* [Factorial](#goto/04-factorial): a recursive and a loop version of the factorial, checked against each other.

Write your own program in your project first. If you get stuck, reveal the example. The source shows the finished program, and the section below says which lines to change for your own version.
