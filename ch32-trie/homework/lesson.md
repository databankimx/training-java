## Homework

This exercise uses a trie to check a dictionary quickly, by asking whether each word's reverse is also a word. Each lookup takes one step for each letter.

The steps are:

* [Reversed words](#goto/01-reversed-words): checking, for each word, whether its reverse is in the dictionary.

Write your own program in your project first. If you get stuck, reveal the example. The source shows the finished program, and the section below says which lines to change for your own version.
