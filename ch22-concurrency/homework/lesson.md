## Homework

These exercises split work between threads. The first shares no data at all, and the second shares a list under a lock. Each one is a step in this folder.

The steps are:

* [Split sum](#goto/01-split-sum): two threads add halves of a range, each writing its own result.
* [Shared list](#goto/02-shared-list): two threads add values to one list, with each add protected by a lock.

Write your own program in your project first. If you get stuck, reveal the example. The source shows the finished program, and the section below says which lines to change for your own version.
