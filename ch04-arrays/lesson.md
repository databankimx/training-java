## Ch04 · Arrays

A program often needs a list of values of one type, such as twelve temperatures or a week of sales. An array stores those values under one name, and an index picks out one of them. This chapter covers how arrays are declared and read, how to create them with values or with defaults, and how to work with grids of values.

The steps are:

* [Declaring arrays](#goto/01-declaring-arrays): declaring an array, reading and changing its elements, and what happens past the end.
* [Initializing arrays](#goto/02-initializing-arrays): starting values, default values, and finding the length.
* [Multidimensional arrays](#goto/03-multidimensional-arrays): arrays of rows, indexed by row and column.
* [Summary](#goto/04-summary): a recap of the chapter and a short quiz. The homework is listed under the chapter.
