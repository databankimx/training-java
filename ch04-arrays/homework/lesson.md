## Homework

These exercises use arrays in the ways the chapter showed: filling them when they're created, reading them by index, and building a grid with two indexes. Each one is a step in this folder.

The steps are:

* [Temperature log](#goto/01-temperature-log): an array of readings, with its first and last values, and their average.
* [Tic-tac-toe board](#goto/02-tic-tac-toe-board): a two-dimensional array that holds the marks on a board.

Write your own program in your project first. If you get stuck, reveal the example. The source shows the finished program, and the section below says which lines to change for your own version.
