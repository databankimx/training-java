## Homework

These exercises use arrays for lists of values: three scores, and the names of the days of the week. Each one is a step in this folder.

The steps are:

* [Highest score](#goto/03-highest-score): an array of three scores, compared to find the highest.
* [Weekday name](#goto/04-weekday-name): an array of day names, indexed by a number the user types, with a range check first.

Write your own program in your project first. If you get stuck, reveal the example. The source shows the finished program, and the section below says which lines to change for your own version.
