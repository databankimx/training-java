## Ch01 · Getting Started

This chapter gets you from nothing to a running Java program. You'll check your setup, write the smallest complete program there is, and learn the pieces it's made from. Along the way you'll see how the runner turns your source into a program, so that when something goes wrong later, you'll know where to look.

The steps are:

* [Set up your workstation](#goto/00-setup): a check that the JDK and the runner are in place. This step has no code.
* [Hello, world](#goto/01-hello-world): a program that prints two lines, and the parts of a Java program.
* [Imports](#goto/02-imports): how `import` brings in other parts of the standard library, and what Java gives you automatically.
* [Comments](#goto/03-comments): how to leave notes for the people who read your code.
* [Summary](#goto/04-summary): a recap of the chapter and a short quiz. The homework is listed under the chapter.

---

*The examples and explanations are new. They follow the C++ course's first chapter, with Java in place of C++.*
