## Homework

These exercises use lambdas with the library's sorting and iteration: sorting words by length, and totalling values as a list is walked. Each one is a step in this folder.

The steps are:

* [Sort by length](#goto/01-sort-by-length): a lambda that sorts words by length, keeping equal lengths in their original order.
* [Count and sum](#goto/02-count-and-sum): a lambda passed to `forEach` that totals the even values in a list.

Write your own program in your project first. If you get stuck, reveal the example. The source shows the finished program, and the section below says which lines to change for your own version.
