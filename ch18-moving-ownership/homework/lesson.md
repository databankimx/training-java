## Homework

These exercises practise handing an object from one holder to another: a method that takes ownership of a resource, and a list that moves a resource in and out. Each one is a step in this folder.

The steps are:

* [Ownership handoff](#goto/01-ownership-handoff): a method that takes a resource from a box, and closes it when it returns.
* [Move through a list](#goto/02-move-through-vector): a resource moved into a list and back out, with the list's emptied box removed.

Write your own program in your project first. If you get stuck, reveal the example. The source shows the finished program, and the section below says which lines to change for your own version.
