## Homework

These exercises use `var` and `Optional` in small programs: printing a sorted map and its total, and a division that reports when there is no answer. Each one is a step in this folder.

The steps are:

* [Word totals](#goto/01-word-totals): a sorted map printed with `var` in its loops, and a total of its values.
* [Safe division](#goto/02-safe-division): a division that returns an `Optional`, with the empty case reported.

Write your own program in your project first. If you get stuck, reveal the example. The source shows the finished program, and the section below says which lines to change for your own version.
