## Homework

These exercises practise catching exceptions from input that may be invalid, and cleaning up after a file. Each one is a step in this folder.

The steps are:

* [Safe sum](#goto/01-safe-sum): add up a list of text values, skipping any that aren't numbers.
* [Read with cleanup](#goto/02-read-with-cleanup): read a file, and always report that it was closed, even when reading fails.

Write your own program in your project first. If you get stuck, reveal the example. The source shows the finished program, and the section below says which lines to change for your own version.
