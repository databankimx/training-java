## Homework

These exercises use patterns to check and read text: a shape check on email addresses, and a sum of prices found in a receipt. Each one is a step in this folder.

The steps are:

* [Check emails](#goto/01-check-emails): a pattern that accepts a simple email address, tested on valid and invalid text.
* [Sum prices](#goto/02-sum-prices): groups that take the dollars and cents from each price, and a total kept in cents.

Write your own program in your project first. If you get stuck, reveal the example. The source shows the finished program, and the section below says which lines to change for your own version.
