## Homework

These exercises practise the chapter's main points: shared references, `null`, and comparing text with `equals`. Each one is a step in this folder.

The steps are:

* [Name tracker](#goto/01-name-tracker): a program that checks a name that may not have been entered yet.
* [Shared scores](#goto/02-shared-scores): two names for one array, and what each one sees after a change.

Write your own program in your project first. If you get stuck, reveal the example. The source shows the finished program, and the section below says which lines to change for your own version.
