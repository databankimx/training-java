## Homework

These exercises practise references in methods, and checking a reference for `null` before it's used. Each one is a step in this folder.

The steps are:

* [Swap with references](#goto/03-swap-with-references): swapping two values through a reference to an array.
* [Safe bonus](#goto/04-safe-bonus): adding a bonus only when there's a score to change.

Write your own program in your project first. If you get stuck, reveal the example. The source shows the finished program, and the section below says which lines to change for your own version.
