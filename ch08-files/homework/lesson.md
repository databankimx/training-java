## Homework

These exercises practise reading and writing files. Each one is a step in this folder.

The steps are:

* [Numbered copy](#goto/03-numbered-copy): reading a file and writing a copy with each line numbered.
* [Word count](#goto/04-word-count): counting the lines and words in a file, using two readers.

Write your own program in your project first. If you get stuck, reveal the example. The source shows the finished program, and the section below says which lines to change for your own version.
