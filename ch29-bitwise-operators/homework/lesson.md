## Homework

These exercises use bit tests to answer questions about a number: how many of its bits are 1, and whether it's a power of two. Each one is a step in this folder.

The steps are:

* [Count set bits](#goto/01-count-set-bits): counting the 1 bits in a number, by clearing the lowest one each pass.
* [Power of two](#goto/02-power-of-two): checking that a number has exactly one bit set.

Write your own program in your project first. If you get stuck, reveal the example. The source shows the finished program, and the section below says which lines to change for your own version.
