## Homework

These exercises practise inheritance and polymorphism: classes that share behavior, and a method that works on any of them. Each one is a step in this folder.

The steps are:

* [Shapes](#goto/03-shapes): a base class with an area method, and subclasses that replace it, printed through one method.
* [Employee pay](#goto/04-employee-pay): an employee and a manager whose pay is calculated differently, printed through one method.

Write your own program in your project first. If you get stuck, reveal the example. The source shows the finished program, and the section below says which lines to change for your own version.
