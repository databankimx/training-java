## Ch12 · Inheritance

Many classes share most of their behavior. A car is a kind of vehicle, and a sports car is a kind of car. This chapter covers how a class can take on the members of another class and add its own, how the access levels apply to inherited members, how a class can take on behavior from interfaces, and how one call can behave differently for each object it's made on.

The steps are:

* [Inheriting members](#goto/01-inheriting-members): `extends`, the base and derived class, and inherited members.
* [Protected members and multiple inheritance](#goto/02-protected-and-multiple): the protected access level, and classes that take on behavior from interfaces.
* [Interfaces](#goto/03-interfaces): declaring an interface, implementing it, and using it as a type.
* [Polymorphism](#goto/04-polymorphism): calling one method through a base type, and the version that runs for each object.
* [Summary](#goto/05-summary): a recap of the chapter and a short quiz. The homework is listed under the chapter.
