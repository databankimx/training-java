## Ch12 · Inheritance

Many classes share parts: a dog and a cat are both animals with a name, and a circle and a square are both shapes with an area. Inheritance lets a new class take on the fields and methods of an existing one, and then add to them or change them. Polymorphism is what makes that useful: a program can treat many kinds of object as one kind, and each object still does its own thing.

The steps are:

* [Extending classes](#goto/01-extending-classes): `extends`, reusing a parent's fields and methods, and calling the parent's constructor with `super`.
* [Overriding and polymorphism](#goto/02-overriding-and-polymorphism): replacing a method in a subclass, and calling it through a parent type.
* [Abstract classes](#goto/03-abstract-classes): classes that describe a shared shape without being created directly.
* [Summary](#goto/04-summary): a recap of the chapter and a short quiz. The homework is listed under the chapter.
