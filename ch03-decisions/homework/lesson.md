## Homework

These exercises put the chapter to work: comparisons, logical operators, `if` chains, and `switch`. Each one is a step in this folder, and the exercise's lesson says what to write.

The steps are:

* [Grade letter](#goto/01-grade-letter): an `if` and `else if` chain that turns a score into a letter.
* [Leap year](#goto/02-leap-year): logical operators that combine three rules into one answer.
* [Operator menu](#goto/03-operator-menu): a `switch` on an operator, with an `if` inside one case.

Write your own program in your project first. If you get stuck, reveal the example. The source shows the finished program, and the section below says which lines to change for your own version.
