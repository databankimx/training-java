## Ch03 · Decisions

Every program makes decisions. It checks whether a value is big enough, whether a name was entered, or whether two things match, and then it chooses what to do next. This chapter covers the boolean values those decisions rest on, the operators that combine them, and the statements that act on them: `if`, `else`, and `switch`.

The steps are:

* [Booleans and comparisons](#goto/01-booleans): the `boolean` type, and the comparison operators that produce it.
* [Logical operators and short-circuiting](#goto/02-logical-operators): combining conditions, the truth tables, and how evaluation stops early.
* [If statements](#goto/03-if-statements): choosing between paths with `if`, `else`, and `else if`.
* [Switch statements](#goto/04-switch): choosing among constant values, including the arrow form.
* [Summary](#goto/05-summary): a recap of the chapter and a short quiz. The homework is listed under the chapter.
