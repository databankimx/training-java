## Ch06 · Loops

A loop repeats a block of code while a condition holds, or once for each value in a collection. This chapter covers the `while` and `for` loops, the enhanced `for` over arrays and collections, `break` and `continue` to change a loop's path, your own classes that work in an enhanced `for`, and a larger program, Battleship, that uses them together.

The steps are:

* [While loops](#goto/01-while-loops): repeating while a condition is true, and the do-while form.
* [For loops](#goto/02-for-loops): a counter with a start, a test, and a step.
* [Enhanced for](#goto/03-enhanced-for): visiting each value of an array or collection.
* [Break and continue](#goto/04-break-and-continue): leaving a loop early, and skipping to its next pass.
* [Custom iterables](#goto/05-custom-iterables): making your own class work in an enhanced `for`.
* [Battleship](#goto/06-battleship): a complete game built from the loops in this chapter.
* [Summary](#goto/07-summary): a recap of the chapter and a short quiz. The homework is listed under the chapter.
