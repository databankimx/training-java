## Homework: Days until

Count the days from today, November 5, 2026, to three dates, and to a date in the past.

Write your own program in your project first. If you get stuck, reveal the example: the source shows the finished program, and the section below says which lines to change for your own version.

The program should:

* create a `LocalDate` for today, and for January 1, 2027, and for February 29, 2028
* print the number of days from today until New Year's Day, and until the leap day
* print the number of days from January 1, 2026 until today

### What to check

The output should be:

```text
Days until New Year: 57
Days until the leap day: 481
Days since New Year 2026: 308
```

The count to January 1, 2027 is 25 days in November, then 31 in December, then one more day. The leap day is in 2028, which is a leap year, so the count across February 2028 includes 29 days.

### Extensions

* Print a negative count by measuring from a later date to an earlier one, and explain what the sign means.
* Find the number of days until your next birthday, using the date from the birthday homework.
