## Homework: Birthday weekday

Find the weekday of a birthday in 2026, and the date and weekday 100 days later. Dates are shown in the US form, with the month first.

Write your own program in your project first. If you get stuck, reveal the example: the source shows the finished program, and the section below says which lines to change for your own version.

The program should:

* create a `LocalDate` for July 4, 2026
* print the date in the US form, and its weekday name in English
* add 100 days, and print the new date in the US form with its weekday name

### What to check

The output should be:

```text
Birthday: 7/4/2026
Weekday: Saturday
100 days later: 10/12/2026 (Monday)
```

Check the weekday by hand before you run the program. Counting the days from a known weekday is a good way to test your understanding of the calendar.

### Extensions

* Change the birthday to your own, and print the weekday of the next birthday.
* Print the weekday for the first day of each month of 2026. Think about which months start on the same weekday.
