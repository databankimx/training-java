## Homework

These exercises use `java.time` to answer questions about the calendar: the weekday of a date, the date some days later, and the number of days until another date. Each one is a step in this folder.

The steps are:

* [Birthday weekday](#goto/01-birthday-weekday): the weekday of a date, and the date 100 days later.
* [Days until](#goto/02-days-until): counting days to New Year's Day, to a leap day, and from the start of the year.

Write your own program in your project first. If you get stuck, reveal the example. The source shows the finished program, and the section below says which lines to change for your own version.
