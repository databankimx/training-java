## Ch31 · Dates and times

Dates look simple, but the calendar has month lengths, leap years, and other rules a program shouldn't work out by hand. This chapter covers the `java.time` package, which handles those rules: dates, dates with times, formatting them as text, and measuring the time between two points.

Dates in this chapter are shown in the US form, with the month first.

The steps are:

* [Dates](#goto/01-dates): creating a date, formatting it, and adding days.
* [Dates and times together](#goto/02-date-and-time): a date and time with no time zone, and formatting both.
* [Measuring time between dates](#goto/03-between-dates): counting days between dates, and durations.
* [Summary](#goto/04-summary): a recap of the chapter and a short quiz. The homework is listed under the chapter.
