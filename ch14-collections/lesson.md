## Ch14 · Collections

Arrays have a fixed size, and that's often the wrong shape for data. A program that reads an unknown number of values, or looks things up by name, needs a collection that grows, and one that finds values by key. This chapter covers lists, which grow and keep their order, and maps, which store values under keys, in both sorted and hash-table forms. It finishes with how to choose between them.

The steps are:

* [Lists](#goto/01-lists): a list that grows, reading and adding elements, and the cost of each operation.
* [Maps](#goto/02-maps): a sorted map that finds values by key.
* [Hash maps](#goto/03-unordered-maps): a map that trades order for faster lookups.
* [Choosing a collection](#goto/04-choosing-collections): the job each collection suits, and what each one costs.
* [Summary](#goto/05-summary): a recap of the chapter and a short quiz. The homework is listed under the chapter.
