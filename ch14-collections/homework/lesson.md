## Homework

These exercises use the collections from this chapter: a list of scores, and a count of words. Each one is a step in this folder.

The steps are:

* [Grade book](#goto/03-grade-book): a list of scores, with its count, highest, lowest, and average.
* [Word tally](#goto/04-word-tally): a sorted map of word counts, and the most common word.

Write your own program in your project first. If you get stuck, reveal the example. The source shows the finished program, and the section below says which lines to change for your own version.
